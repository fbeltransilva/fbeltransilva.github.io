Welcome! My name is Francisco Beltran-Silva, but I go by Franco.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at California State University, Northridge. I am also an Associate Researcher for V-Auction at Vollers Group. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Georgia State University.

Research Interests

  • Health economics
  • Development economics
  • Public economics
  • Adolescent health, substance use, and mental health
  • Crime, informality, and public policy
