Research themes

Policy-relevant research on health and human capital

Current projects focus on adolescent health, social media use, substance use, marijuana legalization, birth outcomes, safety, and labor-market related health behavior.

Adolescent health

Risk behavior and mental health

Research on social media use, substance use, suicide risk, body image, sexual behavior, interpersonal violence, and weapon carrying among U.S. high school students.

Public policy

Taxes, legalization, and health

Projects study tobacco taxation, cannabis liberalization, dispensary access, and their consequences for maternal, infant, and adolescent outcomes.

Development

Violence, poverty, and birth outcomes

Work includes studies of nationwide interventions, Mexico's Drug War, mandatory education, and birth-related outcomes.
