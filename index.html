Applied microeconomics

Development, health, and public economics

Welcome! I am an applied microeconomist with research interests in development, health, and public economics. I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at California State University, Northridge. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Georgia State University.

My research agenda is strongly influenced by my experience in the Ministry of Finance in Mexico, where I worked for over four years assessing the impact of fiscal policies. My work focuses mainly on how public policies affect health-related outcomes, with additional projects on informality, crime, and adolescent well-being.

About me

My name is Francisco, but I go by Franco. I was born and raised in Mexico City and lived for five years in Atlanta, Georgia. Outside of research and teaching, I enjoy discovering new music, listening to film scores, playing piano, learning guitar and ukulele, composing music, cycling, and beach volleyball.
