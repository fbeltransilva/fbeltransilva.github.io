Applied microeconomics

Health and public economics.

Welcome! My name is Francisco Beltran-Silva, but I go by Franco. I am an applied microeconomist with a focus on development, health, and public economics.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at California State University, Northridge. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Georgia State University.

My research agenda focuses mainly on how public policies affect health-related outcomes. My fields of interest are strongly influenced by my experience in Mexico's Ministry of Finance, where I worked for four years assessing the impact of fiscal policies. My work also explores topics related to taxes and violence.

About Me

I was born and raised in Mexico City. Before academia, I worked at the Ministry of Finance in Mexico, where I evaluated fiscal policies and collaborated with representatives from international organizations such as the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund, and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. I also previously worked at Evercore Partners Mexico, an investment bank. Outside of work, I enjoy music, cycling, and beach volleyball. I play piano, electric guitar, and ukelele. I also like composing music. I am currently learning harmonica and drums.
