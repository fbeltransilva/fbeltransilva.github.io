Personal

Creative me

Outside of economics, I have a strong passion for music. I like discovering new artists and love music festivals. I mostly listen to indie music, rock, electronic music, and instrumental music.

I enjoy film scores; Hans Zimmer and Alexander Desplat are among my favorites. I play piano and am currently learning guitar and ukulele. I also enjoy composing music. Besides music, I like being physically active, especially cycling and beach volleyball.

Music and podcast

I sometimes share music and creative projects online. I also have worked on a Spanish-language podcast that approaches the entertainment industry from an economist's perspective.
