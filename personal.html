Creative Me

I have a strong passion for music. I play piano, electric guitar, and ukelele. I am currently learning harmonica and drums. I like discovering new artists and attending music festivals. I mostly listen to indie music, rock, electronic music, and instrumental music. I also love film scores; Hans Zimmer and Alexandre Desplat are among my favorites. I also like composing music, below you can find the link to my soundcloud. I have a podcast in Spanish, named La Payola, where I touch topics about the entertainment industry from the lens of an economist.
