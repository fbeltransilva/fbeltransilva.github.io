Teaching

I am passionate about teaching economics as a powerful tool for understanding and solving real-world problems.

Teaching Philosophy

As an instructor, my approach prioritizes teaching economics as a useful framework for solving different problems while preparing students to be successful in their future careers. I have noticed that professors who leave a lasting impression share several traits: they are knowledgeable, motivating, and able to connect with students both as a group and individually. This appreciation has shaped the foundations of my own teaching philosophy.
