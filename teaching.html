Teaching

Economics as a tool for solving problems

I am passionate about teaching. My approach emphasizes economics as a powerful tool to understand real-world problems while helping students build skills for their future careers.

I have noticed that professors who leave a lasting impression tend to be knowledgeable, motivating, and able to connect with students as a group and individually. That appreciation has shaped my own teaching philosophy.
