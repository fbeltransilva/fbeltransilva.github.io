Economics Tutoring Schedule

Provided by the CSUN Department of Economics

Tuesday, Sept. 04 to Friday, Dec. 04
Tutoring is not available on University Holidays or Thanksgiving Recess
TUTORING PROGRAM GUIDELINES:
NO APPOINTMENT NECESSARY
  • You have read the textbook and tried to solve the questions yourself at least once; tutors will not do assignments for students.
  • You bring your textbook, your notes, the assignments, and your calculator with you.
  • You are courteous and respectful to others.
  • Tutoring sessions occur in a group environment; one-on-one tutoring may occur if there are no other tutees present.

Sign in and out on each visit (paper log sheets are available with the tutor)

Tutors have the right to decline your request for help unless you meet the requirements below for this free tutoring service.
