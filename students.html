Resources I share with students

These notes collect a few resources from my teaching website in a cleaner format. The goal is simple: help students study more effectively, think about economics as a major, and reflect on life after college.

How to Study Better

Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning by Peter C. Brown

I wish I had this book in my hands as a college student. Take advantage of it; it can boost your ability to learn. The book is a useful starting point for students who want practical, evidence-based study techniques.

Interview with Peter Brown Book link Summary of the book Stephen Chew video series

While reading the book is the best way to learn the techniques, students can also benefit from reviewing the main takeaways and from Stephen Chew's video series on how to study.
